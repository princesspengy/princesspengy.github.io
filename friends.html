friends of the site

this page will be where I tell you about my friends and the cool stuff they get up to on the internet!! you should go check em out!!

also they don't really post or have any other notable dwellings on the internet but I'm gonna use this space to shout out my special girl ashley and my sister willow who I both love very very much with all my little alien heart <3

Lore Evermore

my best buddy!! they're an artist, habitual streamer, and general little freak who has some of the best character designs and general artistic sensibilities in the business. they're scattered across a lot of different places on the internet nowadays, which you can all find on their website!

Bee Braun

develops games!! we talk about video games a whole ton and she has an intense thoughtfulness towards the medium that makes her stuff an absolute delight. she has a website you can visit here, and a social media page you can follow here! also go check out the game she's making, stomp and the sword of miracles!

inverseaxolotl

webcomic author, animator, and terrifyingly prolific artist. currently working on all kinds of stories about people being horrible disasters, which you can see most of on bluesky! she also has a sideblog where she draws kinky porny goodness of all kinds as "edith", also on bluesky (18+)!

Ara

a goblin with a talent for grisly monster design, magical worlds, juicy emotionally gut-rending drama, cute girls, and fitting an intimidating amount of eggs inside of her. check out some of her more recent work on bluesky (18+)!


utilitymonstergirl

cartoonist and comic author who deftly balances the erotic, revolting, and hilarious. if queer people ripping each other apart in every way you could possibly interpret this sentence sounds great to you, probably the best collection of her work can be found over on her comicfury page (EXTREMELY 18+), and check out her blog too!
